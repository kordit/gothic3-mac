Native Gothic 3 (Community Patch 1.75.14) for Apple Silicon Macs. The game files come from your own Steam account; the app has none.

- First install: `Gothic3-Mac-<version>.dmg`, drag the app to Applications, then System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway (the app is not notarized).
- Already installed: the launcher offers this update itself.
- `Gothic3Recomp-macOS.zip` is what the launcher's update downloads.

See README.md for what works and what does not yet.
